Anthony Journal.

Notes on consciousness, freedom, discipline, recognition, and the making of a self.

How I used to understand consciousness I used to just disregard the idea of questioning my consciousness, the idea itself would only lead me to more questions and wouldn’t benefit me, but now I am starting to see the benefit. (9/16/2026) Discussion An experience I can relate to reading is my own experience questioning my thoughts at night, staying up late wondering whether or not I could ever definitely know if what I think I know is true. Existential crises from this line of thinking also reminded me of how unsure Descartes felt when he wasn’t certain he as a person existed. But the cogito hypothesis helped quell his fears, and ultimately he and I found ways to satisfy our questioning, albeit not completely. Something I would like to understand better from the reading is the ideas behind which form of relationship you believe the conscious mind and the physical body have, I sort of agreed with one of the examples but I didn't agree completely with. 9/23/2026 Journal Entry The choice to go out and practice jumping up and down on a skateboard (doing ollies) changed me physically and gave me better balance and it did so by training muscles I had never felt as sore before then. It also changed my personality as I had to eat lots of crap and fall down and fail before I could do my ollies the way I wanted to, which in turn changed my outlook and attitude towards life, because I did succeed in the end. I now knew the power of perseverance. 2. I believe that the detachment model relates more firmly to my experience because I often find myself questioning how much my perception even matters which leads my thoughts to dwell more internally on my own struggles. Only once those struggles are dealt with, at least partially, do I begin the more disciplined model for consciousness where I wonder what I value in the world around me, and what am I willing to give up for it. 3. Are we the same people our whole lives — No we are not, unless I can truly attribute something to myself, an essence, for lack of a better word, that is unchanging throughout my entire life, I cannot say that even 1 part of me has remained the same. 4. Reexamining my passage from Hegel makes me further question whether or not we can truly ever perceive ourselves, perhaps Hegel is right and our self-consciousness can only exist in the ideas of us that form in other people's mind, because if there were not other objective observer, we can changing too much breaking our own continuity that we could never achieve a picture of our self, we could never engage in the discussion which hegel notes to describe our people. But it also complicates his argument because if the self is changing, what is simple being-for-itself, as hegel suggests self-consciousness is. Who are we pleasing when we are being just for ourselves, a person that existed seconds maybe years ago, or the person who will exist in the future. 9/28/2026 Discussion: How does Scarry relate to Hegel? Both perform some sort of externalization of the human experience, there is a shared outside world between the two, for hegel it is the other, for Scarry it is the natural world which is inanimate. Both also lean towards the more metaphysical view of the world where there are human things that science cannot describe. They also both recognize the role of pain in shaping ourselves, with hegel’s torture of labor and being a slave ultimately being freeing and scarry having the idea that pain is what truly defines the experience to be us. 10/7/2026 Discussion: Wiggenstein would probably describe consciousness as a set of some kind that describes many connections between varying parts of the body and human concepts, ultimately forming a network with vast overlaps and few conjunctions between different parts, and likely 0 conjunctions where everything meets, where the sum total could be called a consciousness. Wiggenstein would probably say that freedom is the ability to choose disparate parts from all the different strings that make up your network and to form your own definition based on those disparate parts. Freedom is the ability to make up your own definition from all parts of the network and then have your definition also be a part of the network. Wiggenstein would say that our self is sometimes our brain and our active mind, sometimes it is the concept of us that exists in other people's heads, sometimes any of the other ways you could think of yourself. But Wiggenstein might distinguish that it is never NOT any of those ways, it is always a mixture of them all, a network of them all. October 12th 2026, How does our writing relate to westworld: Our writing relates to westworld in that both are explorations of consciousness. Westworld is a TV series while we are writing a paper that explores consciousness. Just like Dolores has to travel down the maze in Westworld, we have to travel down our own rabbit hole in this class seeing our own consciousness in new POVs every week. We both also share the goal to better understand ourselves and our consciousnesses. October 28th 2026. What resonated with me in lecture was the talk about self identity and how we structure ourselves through other people. This is almost definitely because this was leading into talk about Hegel but I'm excited to present next week. November 4th 2026. One place I am having an inner conflict is my existential conflict. A portion of me wants to just give up and choose the easy way by not worrying or striving for a purpose in life whilst the rational mind knows I should keep pursuing the goal of a purpose as even the process itself will reward me with something similar to a purpose. The master is the side of me that is pessimistic and doesn’t want to see things through while the slave is the working side that drags the pessimistic side with him. November 9th 2026, one moment I had that I believe to be like epaminondas is when I undertook the burden of skateboarding. The unrelenting will you have to keep on picking yourself up even after falling on your ass hundreds of times is something I want to always remember. Its determination that I learned and I feel like I wasn’t as determined before. That and the fact that you dont simply learn how to do something once, you must repeat your training and fall after youve landed once, because otherwise you will not land again. I came to believe more in myself and what I could accomplish, I also learned my limits at the same time but relentlessness effort is something that will always help me now, effort is now part of me and I enjoy that change.
