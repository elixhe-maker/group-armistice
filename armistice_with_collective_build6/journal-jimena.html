How does Detachment or discipline (models for achieving consciousness) relate to your experience. Is one more applicable than the other? I think it is important to maintain discipline in order to work towards achieving consciousness. Discipline helps you stay more focused towards your goal and ignore the surrounding “noise” around you. I feel like detachment is a much harder method for achieving consciousness, I get attached to things very easily and even though it might help achieve consciousness, it might not help. Are we the same person throughout our lives? I don't think we remain the same person throughout our lifespan. Although we remain in the same body, our consciousness evolves through experiences we have, our cells also regenerate which create different versions of us compared to when we were kids. As one experiences so many things throughout our lifetimes, those experiences come to change how we act and who we are as a person.
