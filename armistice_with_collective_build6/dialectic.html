THE PHILOSOPHY BEHIND THE GAME

Why does the master lose?

In Hegel's account, the master receives recognition from a subordinated consciousness, making that recognition unstable. The bondsman, meanwhile, transforms the world through labor and develops a different kind of self-relation. The game simplifies this dialectic to let you test the tensions between domination, work, and mutual recognition.
