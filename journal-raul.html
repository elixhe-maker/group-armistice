How does Scarry relate to Hegel? They both can be related in the sense that they both depend on another “thing” to achieve a form of consciousness. That is to say, they have to mediate themselves either through another object or an external “other”. For Hegel, self-consciousness emerges through labor and the mutual recognition between subjects, which consist of the working material of the world reflects one’s agency back to themselves. Likewise, Scarry shows that internal human vulnerability and intention must be externalized into created artifacts (understood as the “making” of our shared world) without which consciousness collapses inward, as seen when severe pain obliterates language and thought.
